Before you ride

  • In Washington, Sur-Rons and Talarias count as off-road motorcycles, not e-bikes. They need a WA ORV tag from the Department of Licensing.
  • Only ride trails and roads marked open to motorcycles. Mountain bike and hiking trails are off limits.
  • Forest Service trailheads need a Northwest Forest Pass or the day fee. DNR land (Naneum Ridge, Beverly Dunes) needs a Discover Pass.
  • Not street legal: you can't ride on paved county roads to get to a trailhead.
